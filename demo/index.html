TriggerMap · demo

Under what conditions is a vulnerability reachable?

TriggerMap reads English security advisories for Python packages and suggests the deployment conditions they state: a setting, a feature in use, the attacker's access, a platform. Each item is a verbatim span of the advisory, with its role and logic, for a person to confirm or correct. These are three unedited outputs of the released model: one ordinary case, one hard case and one abstention.

Flask session cookie disclosure

GHSA-m2qf-hxjv-5gpq

The advisory lists five conditions that must all hold. The model finds two of them. It misses both required absences ("does not access or modify the session", "does not set a Cache-Control header") and the default-on SESSION_REFRESH_EACH_REQUEST, and it wrongly marks a background phrase as a requirement.

Django MultiPartParser traversal

GHSA-xgxc-v2qg-chmh

The model finds no conditions, and the page says so plainly, without claiming the conditions do not apply. Both model drafts of this advisory mark the MultiPartParser requirement and the carve-out "built-in upload handlers were not affected"; the model finds neither.

Open the review page →
  • Model: the CRF crf-batches-01-02 from release v0.1.0, with review policy review-policy-0. The policy is not calibrated, so every item is marked review required. All three advisories are dev documents; none was used for training.
  • Extraction supports a review. It is not a judgement of whether a deployment is vulnerable, and "no explicit conditions found" is not "not affected".
  • Measured quality is low and is reported with intervals in the repository's README. On the 10 hand-labelled dev documents, typed-condition F1 is 0.370 [0.000, 0.640]. Larger dev sets exist only as model drafts, which measure agreement, not accuracy.
  • The pages are static. Answers you click stay in your browser until you export them, and nothing is sent anywhere.
